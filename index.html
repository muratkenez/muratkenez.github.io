Murat Can Kenez

PhD candidate, Chemical Engineering and Applied Chemistry, University of Toronto

I work on process modeling and techno-economic analysis (TEA) of electrochemical CO2 conversion systems, including direct air capture (DAC) integration, CO2 electrolyzer design and ethylene production.
